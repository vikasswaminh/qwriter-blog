<h2 id="faq" class="os-h2" style="margin-top: 32px; margin-bottom: 24px; color: var(--ink-black); font-size: 2rem; font-weight: 800;">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<summary>Is agentic AI the same thing as an AI agent?</summary>
<div class="faq-content">
<p style="margin: 0;">Not exactly. Agentic AI is a broader approach, systems built to perceive, reason, and act toward a goal. An AI agent is one specific instance built that way, scoped to a particular job.</p>
</div>
</details><details class="os-faq-item">
<summary>How is agentic AI different from generative AI?</summary>
<div class="faq-content">
<p style="margin: 0;">Generative AI creates content and stops. Agentic AI keeps going, perceiving, planning, and acting through real tools until the goal is met, often without being prompted at each step.</p>
</div>
</details><details class="os-faq-item">
<summary>Does Agentic AI mean no human oversight?</summary>
<div class="faq-content">
<p style="margin: 0;">No. The reliable pattern is approval gated autonomy. Agents handle research and drafting on their own, but anything that touches a customer, money, or the public internet needs human sign off first.</p>
</div>
</details><details class="os-faq-item">
<summary>What kinds of businesses benefit most?</summary>
<div class="faq-content">
<p style="margin: 0;">Any business with recurring, well defined, manual tasks. Smaller teams often benefit more since they can't easily absorb repetitive work the way larger teams can.</p>
</div>
</details><details class="os-faq-item">
<summary>What's the biggest mistake businesses make?</summary>
<div class="faq-content">
<p style="margin: 0;">Automating a process that was never clearly defined, and later assuming "autonomous" means it no longer needs supervision.</p>
</div>
</details><details class="os-faq-item">
<summary>How is agentic AI different from traditional automation?</summary>
<div class="faq-content">
<p style="margin: 0;">Automation follows fixed rules with no interpretation. Agentic AI reasons through situations nobody explicitly planned for and decides on a sensible response using judgment.</p>
</div>
</details><details class="os-faq-item"><summary>Is 2026 different, or just more hype?</summary><div class="faq-content"><p class="html-content" style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Yes, genuinely.
